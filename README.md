## Controls & Gameplay
* <img src="https://github.com/Furkan5E/Zombie.JS/blob/master/assets/zombies/zombie.png" width="18" height="14" /> **Movement:** Use `W` `A` `S` `D` to navigate the map.
* <img src="https://github.com/Furkan5E/Zombie.JS/blob/master/assets/zombies/fastZombie.png" width="18" height="14" /> **Combat:** Use your mouse to aim and `Left Click` to shoot.
* <img src="https://github.com/Furkan5E/Zombie.JS/blob/master/assets/zombies/strongZombie.png" width="18" height="14" /> **Survival:** You have exactly 3 lives. If they catch you, it's game over.
* <img src="https://github.com/Furkan5E/Zombie.JS/blob/master/assets/zombies/bossZombie.png" width="18" height="14" /> **Enemies:** Every zombie variant behaves uniquely. Watch your back.
* <img src="https://github.com/Furkan5E/Zombie.JS/blob/master/assets/zombies/splitZombie.png" width="18" height="14" /> **Victory:** Survive all 20 waves to win the game.
